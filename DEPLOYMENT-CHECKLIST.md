# QYXERA v12 deployment checklist

- Upload the full folder so `assets/`, `style.css`, `script.js`, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, and `site.webmanifest` remain together.
- Test `contact.html` once and activate the FormSubmit receiving email if prompted.
- Replace canonical `.html` URLs if your production host rewrites pages to clean URLs.
- Confirm Facebook/social URLs and business contact details before launch.
- Privacy/Terms are general templates; review them against the final analytics, cookies, third-party tools, and legal requirements used in production.
- Client-specific case studies/results can be added later only with approved names, screenshots, and metrics.
- Test desktop widths 1440/1280/1024 and mobile widths 430/390/375.
